# Ryna Mods

The in-game mods for [Ryna](https://ryna.io/wow), a companion for World of Warcraft: Forever.

Ryna is two halves: addons that run in the game, and a small desktop app for the computer you play
on. This organisation is where the addons are published for addon managers. Everything here is
built from one public source, [MaddisonM79/ryna_agent](https://github.com/MaddisonM79/ryna_agent),
which is also where the app lives, where the code is read and changed, and where issues go.

## The mods

| Mod | What it does | Needs the app |
|---|---|---|
| **Ryna Core** | The window, settings and plumbing every mod builds on. `/ryna` opens it. | No |
| **Meter** | A live meter (damage, healing and more) and a threat bar, read from the game itself. | No |
| **Automation** | Does the chores you tick: quests, vendors, mail, loot rolls and the rest. Off until you turn it on. | No |
| **Prices** | What a vendor pays and what the auction house asks, on every item tooltip. | For everyone's pooled prices |
| **Chronicle** | Records your characters, bags, quests, kills and loot. | To see them on ryna.io |
| **Fights** | Your recent fights, moment by moment, in Ryna's window. | Yes |

Every mod needs Ryna Core. Nothing leaves your computer unless you install the app and sign in.
